# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a friendly place for everyone, whether you are
reporting your first bug or have been writing homebrew for years. Everyone
who takes part in this project, online or off, is expected to help keep it
that way.

## Our standard

Behave in a way that keeps the community welcoming:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone starts
  somewhere.
- Keep feedback constructive. When you point out a problem, say what it is
  and, if you can, how it might be solved.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Technical debates are healthy;
  personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith. A short or bluntly worded message is usually a
  language or time issue, not an insult.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated anywhere in this project: harassment of
any kind, personal or political attacks, insults, discriminatory jokes or
language about others' identity (including but not limited to race,
religion, gender, sexuality, or disability), publishing others' private
information without consent, or any other conduct that a reasonable person
would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

These rules apply to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, code
review comments, commit messages, release notes, and any space where you
represent the project.

## Enforcement

If someone violates this code, the maintainers may edit or remove the
content, and may temporarily or permanently ban the person responsible,
depending on the severity and context. Enforcement decisions are explained
to the person involved.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any concerns
about how this code of conduct is applied, please contact the maintainer,
[@AldySan](https://github.com/AldySan), through any contact channel listed
on their GitHub profile, or flag it to a maintainer in a private message
on GitHub. All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion. Reports
are never dismissed for being "too small", and reporting in good faith is
never held against you.
